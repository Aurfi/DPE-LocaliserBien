Supplemental notices for the byte-preserved production stats.html visualization

The production stats.html includes Rollup Plugin Visualizer's treemap viewer and
its bundled frontend library implementations. These supplemental full license
texts accompany the captured bytes; they do not modify the captured HTML or
any hashed application asset. Keep the application's existing Open Licence,
MIT, and ISC notices and its embedded comments as well.

The viewer runtime is exactly byte-identical to the dist/lib/treemap.js shipped
in the locally available rollup-plugin-visualizer 6.0.3 package. That match is not
a claim that its version, or every transitive frontend dependency version, has
been uniquely recovered from production. Package identities below are supported
by the runtime code and matching viewer metadata. Unknown historical versions
are intentionally not assigned.

The upstream source revisions recorded for these notices identify where the
license text was obtained, not the versions bundled in the captured app.
D3's component-specific copyright lines and complete permission/disclaimer
texts are preserved individually rather than replaced by a synthesized
project-wide license. The bytes notice is copied from the complete upstream
notice preserved in Playwright's ThirdPartyNotices.txt; the section's version
identifies that notice source only.

Separate existing uncertainty: the application's legal page attributes its SVG
map artwork generally to Wikimedia Commons, without naming each source work,
author, or exact license. These software notices do not resolve that artwork
provenance or establish that the Open Licence for data covers the artwork.
